The screenshots show the real shipped UI, captured from the built extension against a fixture feed. If you want the
feed shot to show your own content, take a 1280x800 screenshot of your logged-in feed with highlights visible and
replace `screenshot-1-feed.png`; the store only cares about the dimensions.

Both stores reject images with a different aspect ratio, so keep every replacement at the exact size above.
